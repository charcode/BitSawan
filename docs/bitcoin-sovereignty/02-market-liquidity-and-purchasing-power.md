# Market, Liquidity, and Purchasing-Power Risk

[← Back to the framework](README.md)

## Objective

Bitcoin may protect against long-term monetary debasement while remaining highly volatile over shorter periods. The objective is not to eliminate price fluctuation; it is to prevent volatility, debt, or life events from taking away the holder's freedom to choose when to hold or sell.

## Risk register

| Scenario | Early warning | Preventive control | Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forced sale during drawdown | Fiat runway shrinking; large known bill | Emergency reserve outside bitcoin | Cut optional spending; restore runway; sell deliberately only if required |
| Leveraged liquidation | Margin ratio or collateral alerts | Do not pledge deep reserve; avoid leverage | Add no emotional risk; close or reduce exposure under predefined rules |
| Excess concentration | Sleep, plans, or obligations depend on price | Written allocation and liability map | Rebalance gradually with tax and liquidity awareness |
| Yield/counterparty loss | Unclear borrower, rehypothecation, unusually high return | Keep reserve unencumbered and self-custodied | Stop new deposits; preserve contracts/records; attempt lawful withdrawal |
| Currency mismatch | Fiat liabilities due while assets are volatile | Match near-term liabilities with liquid currency | Ring-fence required cash before discretionary investment |
| Fee/liquidity shock | Urgent small UTXOs; mempool congestion | Maintain appropriately sized UTXOs and a fee reserve | Delay if possible; use fee tools carefully; avoid panic consolidation |
| Emotional capitulation or mania | Compulsive price checking; abandoning rules | Pre-committed decision policy and cooling-off period | Pause 24–72 hours; consult designated rational counterparty |

## Personal balance-sheet protocol

### 1. Identify obligations

List taxes, debt service, housing, health, business commitments, dependants, and planned major purchases by date and currency. Do not count expected bitcoin appreciation as liquidity.

### 2. Establish runway

Hold sufficient ordinary liquidity to cover foreseeable needs and a genuine emergency without requiring a bitcoin sale during a severe drawdown. The right period is personal; it should reflect income stability, dependants, health, insurance, location, and business risk.

### 3. Divide holdings by horizon

- money required soon is not deep reserve;
- working capital must remain usable for its intended purpose;
- only capital with a compatible time horizon should absorb Bitcoin volatility;
- bitcoin used as collateral is no longer fully sovereign reserve.

### 4. Define decision rules

Write down legitimate sale reasons: planned life expenditure, portfolio risk reduction, tax, business capital, or an emergency. Define unacceptable reasons: social-media panic, a prediction, revenge trading, or a lender's forced liquidation.

### 5. Stress-test

Model at least: a 50–80% drawdown, two years without recovery, loss of primary income, a major tax bill, urgent medical costs, exchange disruption, and temporarily high fees. The aim is not to forecast; it is to discover forced actions.

## Accumulation protocol

1. Confirm emergency runway is intact.
2. Use only an amount consistent with the stated horizon and concentration limit.
3. Record acquisition date, quantity, fees, source, and cost basis.
4. Withdraw batches when exchange exposure exceeds the operational threshold, balancing fees and UTXO size.
5. Verify the self-custody receive address and later confirm the UTXO in the intended wallet.
6. Avoid letting regular purchasing create one repeatedly reused receive address or many economically uneconomic UTXOs.

## Sale protocol

1. State the purpose and required net amount.
2. Review tax, provenance, privacy, exchange, and banking implications before moving coins.
3. Select UTXOs deliberately; avoid revealing the deep reserve unnecessarily.
4. Test the chosen venue and banking route with a smaller transaction if unused or stale.
5. Use limit/order execution appropriate to liquidity rather than a rushed market sale.
6. Preserve transaction IDs, trade confirmations, fees, and fiat receipt evidence.
7. Reconcile the remaining wallet and update labels.

## Emergency liquidity protocol

When cash is urgently needed:

1. Confirm the amount and deadline; separate urgent need from desired comfort.
2. Use existing fiat liquidity first according to the plan.
3. Compare a controlled bitcoin sale with debt, penalties, or forced collateralisation.
4. If selling, expose only the required UTXO/value and use an already tested route.
5. Do not enter leverage or an unknown lending product merely to avoid the emotion of selling.

## Validation tests

- A deep drawdown does not create insolvency or forced liquidation.
- Near-term obligations are covered in the currencies in which they are due.
- No deep-reserve bitcoin is pledged or earning opaque yield.
- The holder knows how to lawfully convert a limited amount in more than one practical way.
- Acquisition and disposal records can be reconstructed without the exchange account.

## Residual risks

Holding liquidity has inflation and opportunity costs. Diversification can reduce upside as well as downside. Self-custody reduces counterparty exposure but does not remove price risk. The correct allocation is a personal financial decision, not a Bitcoin security constant.
