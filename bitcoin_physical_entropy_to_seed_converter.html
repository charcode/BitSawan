Only position-valid words from the selected embedded dictionary can be entered. For BIP39, the final word is checksum-filtered after word 11/23. For a canonical Trezor 20-word SLIP39 Single-share Backup, words 3–4 are fixed 1-of-1 metadata, word 5 is padding-constrained, and checksum words 18–20 are generated and locked automatically.

Dice/coin/card input is hashed deterministically. BIP39 and Trezor SLIP39 phrase inputs are validated according to their source standard. Converted 12/24-word phrases are deterministic new BIP39 wallets unless the selected source is already that exact BIP39 phrase.

Cards mode: With replacement is the default: return the card and thoroughly reshuffle before every draw, so repeats are allowed and each ideal independent draw contributes log₂(52) ≈ 5.70 bits. About 23 draws exceed 128 bits and 45 exceed 256 bits. Without replacement uses a single shuffled deck; cards cannot repeat and a full 52-card ordering contains about 225.58 bits.
